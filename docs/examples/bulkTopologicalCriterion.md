# Bulk HC / DDC cages

Hexagonal and double-diamond cages on the shipped cells are [Bulk ice and hydrate cages](../tutorials/bulk-ice.md). The shapes are [Prisms and cages](../explanation/cages.md).

The engine tutorial is [Bulk ice](https://docs.dseams.info/tutorials/bulk-ice.html). The bond-graph flags are in the [seams CLI reference](https://docs.dseams.info/reference/cli.html).
