# Paper figures

The d-SEAMS 2.0 paper, *Incremental ring and cage algorithms for ice structure analysis*, is rebuilt from [d-SEAMS/dseams2_repro](https://github.com/d-SEAMS/dseams2_repro). That repository is the source of the paper figures. This wiki does not redraw them.

What the tutorials do run is the same family of analyses on small public inputs:

- bulk ice Ih and ice Ic, and the sI, sII, and sH cages, in [Bulk ice and hydrate cages](../tutorials/bulk-ice.md)
- prism blocks in [Ice nanotubes](../tutorials/nanotubes.md)
- a square sheet in [Monolayer rings](../tutorials/monolayer.md)
- CHILL+ beside q6 in [CHILL+ and q6](../tutorials/order-parameters.md)
- a basal front in [Ice/water interface](../tutorials/interface.md)
- the largest cage cluster in [Nucleation](../tutorials/nucleation.md)

Those figures are produced by the code blocks on each page. They are the small-input checks.

How to cite both papers is on [Publications](../reference/publications.md).
