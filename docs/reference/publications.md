## Criteria named in these pages

- Nguyen and Molinero, CHILL+. *J. Phys. Chem. B* 2015. doi [10.1021/jp510289t](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp510289t).
- Lechner and Dellago, the neighbour-averaged Steinhardt parameter. *J. Chem. Phys.* 129, 114707 (2008). doi [10.1063/1.2977970](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2977970).
- Franzblau, primitive rings. *Phys. Rev. B* 44, 4925 (1991). doi [10.1103/PhysRevB.44.4925](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.44.4925).

The cage coordinate used for nucleation follows the topological network criterion of the d-SEAMS papers above. Stoddard and Ford, *J. Comput. Phys.* 27, 291 (1977), is the cluster decomposition that size is computed with.
